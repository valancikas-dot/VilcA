🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 6, 2025

Summary: We collect only the data necessary for the app to function. We do not sell your data. You can delete your data at any time.

1. Data We Collect

Rangis collects the following data:

  • Account data: email address, name (optional)
  • Project data: project names, estimates, materials, task lists
  • Photos: construction photos you upload
  • Technical data: device type, OS version, crash data

2. How We Use Data

  • Cloud synchronization between devices
  • PDF report generation
  • Account management and authentication
  • App improvement and bug fixing

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secure servers (Firebase/Google Cloud). Data is encrypted during transfer and at rest.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties, except:

  • With your explicit consent
  • When required by law
  • Technical providers (Firebase, Google Analytics) – only anonymous usage data

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data
  • Correct data
  • Delete data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Export data
  • Withdraw consent

6. Contact

Questions about privacy? Contact us:

Email: statyburangis@gmail.com

7. Changes

We may update this policy. Significant changes will be announced in the app.
