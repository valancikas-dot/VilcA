📸 Before/After Photo Documentation

Learn how to professionally document work with photos – for client presentations, portfolio, or dispute resolution.

1

Create Photo Spot

Create a "photo spot" in your project – specific location to photograph (e.g., "Kitchen" or "Bedroom window").

2

Take "Before" Photo

Before starting work, take photo from specific angle. App automatically records date and time.

3

Document Progress

During work, add more photos. You'll have complete progress history.

4

Take "After" Photo

After work is complete, take photo from the same angle. App will show side-by-side comparison.

5

Export Comparison

Generate professional PDF with before/after comparison. Great for portfolio or client presentation.

💡 Pro tip: Always photograph from the same angle and in similar lighting – comparison will be much more impressive!
