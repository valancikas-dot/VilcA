❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Rangis cost?

Rangis Premium costs €9.99 per month. Annual plan – €99.99/year (2 months free). There is a 7-day free trial.

Is there a free version?

Yes, the free version allows 1 project and 2 workers. For unlimited use – Premium subscription.

How do I sync data between devices?

Premium users have automatic cloud synchronization. Data syncs between phone, tablet, and web app.

Can I export reports?

Yes, Premium users can generate PDF reports with all work data, photos, and estimates.

Does the app work offline?

Yes, you can work without internet. Data automatically syncs when you reconnect.

How do I add workers?

In project settings, click "Add worker" and enter their email. They will receive an invitation.

Can I use on multiple devices?

Yes, one subscription works on all your devices – phone, tablet, computer.

How do I cancel subscription?

Through your Google Play or App Store settings. Data remains after cancellation.

Is there training or support?

Yes, we have video tutorials and email support statyburangis@gmail.com. Premium users have priority response.

Can I import data from other apps?

Currently you can import Excel files with materials and estimates. Export to PDF, Excel.
