📖 How to Use Electronic Logbook

Learn how to maintain complete installation history and never miss a scheduled inspection.

📅

Inspection Schedule

Automatic reminders for periodic inspections

📜

Complete History

Notifications

Never miss an upcoming inspection

1

View Installation Timeline

Open any installation to see complete timeline: initial commissioning, periodic tests, modifications, all in chronological order.

2

Set Inspection Schedule

Based on installation type and requirements, set periodic inspection intervals (e.g., every 5 years for domestic, every year for commercial).

3

Receive Reminders

Get notifications before scheduled inspections are due. Plan your work in advance.

4

Access Document Archive

All generated protocols, certificates, and uploaded documents are stored and easily searchable.

5

Export for Authorities

When authorities request documentation, export complete installation history with all supporting documents.

💡 Pro tip: Upload photos of distribution boards and circuit layouts – they'll be invaluable for future inspections!
