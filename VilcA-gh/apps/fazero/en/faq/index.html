❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What standards does Fazero support?

Fazero supports IEC 60364 (HD 60364) international standard for low-voltage electrical installations and local national standards.

How much does Fazero cost?

Fazero Premium costs €14.99 per month. Annual plan – €149.99/year (2 months free). There is a 14-day free trial.

Can I use Fazero offline?

Yes, all measurements and protocols can be created offline. Data automatically syncs when you reconnect to internet.

What measurement types are supported?

Insulation resistance, earth continuity, loop impedance, RCD testing, and other measurements required by IEC 60364.

When will Fazero be available?

Fazero is currently in development and planned for launch in 2025. Sign up for notifications on our website.

Can multiple electricians use one account?

Yes, Premium subscription includes unlimited team members. Each member has their own login.

How are protocols generated?

After entering all measurements, click "Generate Protocol". You get a professional PDF document with all data and compliance confirmation.

Can I customize protocol templates?

Yes, you can add company logo, contact information, and customize protocol layout.

Is my data secure?

Yes, all data is encrypted and stored on secure servers. We comply with GDPR requirements.

Can I import data from other apps?

We plan to support import from common formats. Export to PDF, Excel, and CSV will be available.
