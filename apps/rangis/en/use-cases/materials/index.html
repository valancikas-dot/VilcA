🧱 How to Track Construction Materials

Learn how to effectively track material consumption, avoid shortages, and generate automatic shopping lists.

1

Create Material List

In your project, create a list of all needed materials. Enter name, planned quantity, and measurement unit (m², pcs, kg, etc.).

2

Record Purchases

When you buy materials, enter into app: quantity, price, supplier. You'll know exactly how much you've spent.

3

Track Consumption

After using materials on site, update consumed quantity. App automatically calculates remaining amount.

4

Generate Shopping List

When materials run low, app automatically generates shopping list with needed quantities.

5

Analyze Reports

See consumption reports: which materials were used most, where are the overruns, plan for future projects.

💡 Pro tip: Set alerts when materials drop below minimum – you'll always know when to order!
