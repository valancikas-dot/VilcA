📏 How to Record Electrical Measurements

Learn how to efficiently record all required electrical measurements for IEC 60364 compliance.

Supported Measurement Types

Insulation Resistance

L-N, L-PE, N-PE measurements in MΩ

Earth Continuity

Protective conductor continuity in Ω

Loop Impedance

Earth fault loop impedance Zs in Ω

RCD Testing

Trip time and trip current

Polarity Check

Correct wiring verification

Voltage Measurement

Supply voltage at origin and endpoints

1

Select Circuit

In your installation, select the circuit you're testing (e.g., "Kitchen sockets", "Lighting circuit 1").

2

Choose Measurement Type

Select which measurement you're performing. App will show required minimum/maximum values for that measurement type.

3

Enter Measurement Value

Enter the measured value. You can also record test instrument serial number for traceability.

4

Review Automatic Validation

Fazero instantly validates the measurement against IEC 60364 requirements. Pass/fail is clearly indicated.

5

Add Notes if Needed

For failed measurements or special cases, add notes explaining the situation or remedial actions.

💡 Pro tip: Save your test instrument details once – they'll be automatically added to all measurements!
