📄 How to Generate Test Protocols

Learn how to generate professional PDF test protocols that meet IEC 60364 documentation requirements.

Protocol Contains:

  • Installation details and location
  • Visual inspection results
  • All measurement results with pass/fail status
  • Test instrument details
  • Inspector information and signature
  • Overall compliance declaration
  • Next inspection due date
1

Complete All Required Tests

Before generating protocol, ensure all required tests are completed. App shows progress and missing tests.

2

Review All Results

Check all measurements and test results. Address any failures – either remediate and retest, or add explanatory notes.

3

Enter Inspector Details

Add inspector name, certification/license number, and company details. These appear on the official protocol.

4

Add Recommendations

Enter any recommendations for the installation owner, limitations, or conditions of the installation.

5

Generate PDF Protocol

Click "Generate Protocol". You get a professional PDF document ready for printing or email delivery.

6

Archive and Distribute

Protocol is automatically saved to installation history. Share via email or print for client and authorities.

💡 Pro tip: Add your company logo and contact details in settings – they'll appear on all protocols automatically!
